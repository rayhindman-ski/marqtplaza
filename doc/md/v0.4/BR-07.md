# BR-07 — Trust and freshness information

## Enhancement assessment

**Priority:** P0 — Release gate

**Criticality:** Critical

**Complexity:** High

**Estimated effort:** 12–20 person-days (provisional)

This estimate covers the full existing implementation specification, not only copy: field-level provenance, dates, statuses and caveats, stale/conflicting/unknown states, result and detail presentation, correction/report handling, privacy-aware evidence, bilingual behavior, and acceptance testing. Risk is critical because overstated or stale sensitive claims can mislead decisions; complexity is high because trust semantics must remain faithful from data contracts through moderation states to accessible UI. P0 is a proposed release gate for affected v0.4 journeys, not a verified production emergency or user-approved deadline.

**Work packages**

- Trust vocabulary, field model, and UX design: **3–5 person-days**
- Card/detail provenance and caveat implementation: **4–6 person-days**
- Correction flow and exceptional-state integration: **3–5 person-days**
- Localization, accessibility, and trust-state QA: **2–4 person-days**

**Estimation basis and exclusions:** The range excludes global rollout or dataset acquisition, ongoing verification operations, and third-party or legal lead times. Existing core search, authentication, and data contracts are presumed reusable; if they are absent or materially incompatible, re-estimate. Person-days are combined design, engineering, and QA effort, not elapsed delivery time, and overlap across BRs must not be summed blindly. ## Status and authority

- **Status:** Proposed implementation specification; not implemented or verified behavior.
- **Source of truth:** `requirements.md`, BR-07.
- **Business requirement:** “MarqtPlaza must provide checked-on dates, sources, verification status, and appropriate caveats for sensitive claims so consumers can assess the reliability and freshness of information.”
- The live consumer stack, source records, moderation process, and API contracts are unknown and must be inspected during implementation.
- The workspace report viewer and weekend guide are not the live consumer application or evidence of live trust behavior.
- Proposed fields and endpoints must be reconciled with existing contracts.
- No unsupported verification badge or allergy-safety guarantee may be introduced.

## Objective

Let consumers judge each material claim by exposing field-level provenance, checked-on dates, status, and caveats in understandable Dutch and English, while representing missing evidence explicitly as unknown.

## Scope

- Trust metadata on result cards and place details.
- Field-level source, checked-on date, and status for opening hours, address, category, accessibility, dietary, price, and other decision-relevant claims when available.
- Plain-language caveats for sensitive or changeable claims.
- Explicit current Hague-only availability and distinction among locally held records, opted-in web results, consumer submissions, and merchant-provided information.
- Stale, conflicting, unknown, unavailable, corrected, and pending-review states.
- Correction/report flow with privacy-aware evidence handling.

## Non-goals

- Certifying a business, person, product, food, accessibility condition, or safety outcome.
- Guaranteeing allergen absence, opening status, availability, price, or suitability.
- Treating source presence as verification.
- Inventing dates, statuses, or provenance when absent.
- Publishing internal risk scores or private moderation notes.
- Showing a single blanket badge that obscures field-level differences.

## Trust vocabulary
| Proposed status | Meaning | Required presentation |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | Evidence was checked under a defined method | Source, checked-on date, field, and method summary |
| Reported | Supplied by a named source but not independently confirmed | Source and caveat |
| Pending review | New/conflicting input awaits review | Do not present as confirmed |
| Stale | Older than the field-specific freshness policy | Date and warning |
| Conflicting | Credible sources disagree | Competing values/sources or neutral warning |
| Unknown | Evidence or value is unavailable | Explicit `Unknown`; no inferred value |
| Removed | Value was withdrawn or invalidated | Do not continue showing it as active |

These statuses are proposed and must be aligned with actual governance and contracts.
## Target screens

1. **Results list:** compact source-group heading and essential date/status summary.
2. **Place detail:** field-level trust information and expandable source details.
3. **Sensitive-claim caveat:** adjacent to dietary, allergy, accessibility, or safety-relevant claims.
4. **Source details panel:** source name/type, checked-on date, status, and non-sensitive method summary.
5. **Report a correction:** selected field, suggested correction, optional evidence, consent notice, and confirmation.
6. **Conflicting/stale state:** visible warning and practical next action, such as contacting the place.

## Interaction flows

### Flow 1 — Assess a result

1. **Entry:** Consumer reaches local list results.
2. **UI response:** Results identify their source group and show essential status/date information.
3. **Action:** Consumer opens a place.
4. **Detail response:** Material fields expose source, checked-on date, and status; absent metadata reads `Unknown`.
5. **Back:** List criteria, source scope, and position are restored.
6. **Failure:** Metadata load failure is named; core factual value is not silently relabelled as confirmed.

### Flow 2 — Opt into web results

1. **Entry:** Fresh session is local-only.
2. **Action:** Consumer explicitly selects **Include web results**.
3. **UI response:** Scope explanation updates and results are grouped by local index versus web source.
4. **Persisted state:** Public `scope` is added to the canonical URL; provider tokens are excluded.
5. **Cancel:** Turning opt-in off removes web groups without changing other criteria.
6. **Retry:** Provider failure offers Retry for that group while local results remain usable.

### Flow 3 — Review sensitive information

1. **Entry:** Consumer encounters an allergy, dietary, accessibility, or safety-relevant field.
2. **UI response:** Claim is paired with source/date/status and an appropriate caveat.
3. **Action:** Consumer opens source details or a contact action.
4. **Outcome:** UI advises direct confirmation when information may change.
5. **Undesired prevention:** No “safe,” “guaranteed,” or verification badge appears without an approved evidence policy.

### Flow 4 — Report a correction

1. **Entry:** Consumer selects **Report an issue** beside a field.
2. **Action:** Consumer chooses field, enters correction, and optionally supplies evidence.
3. **UI response:** Privacy/retention notice appears before submission.
4. **Success:** Receipt/reference is shown; published value remains at its current status until review.
5. **Cancel:** Draft is discarded or retained only with explicit consumer choice.
6. **Error/retry:** Input remains locally available for retry without duplicate submission.

### Flow 5 — Handle stale or conflicting data

1. **Entry:** Field crosses freshness policy or sources conflict.
2. **UI response:** Status changes to stale/conflicting with dates and sources.
3. **Action:** Consumer can inspect detail, contact the place, or report newer evidence.
4. **Resolution:** A reviewed update changes only the affected field and retains audit history internally.

## State requirements

| State | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Initial | Trust labels have plain-language definitions available. |
| Loading | Trust placeholders are not styled as confirmed facts; status is announced. |
| Success | Field-level source/date/status are rendered consistently. |
| Empty | `Unknown` is visible when evidence is absent; the field is not guessed. |
| Error | Metadata failure is distinguished from place/result failure and supports Retry. |
| Permission denied | Trust information remains available; location permission is irrelevant. |
| Guest | Can view trust details and submit a correction under disclosed terms. |
| Account | Can receive permitted correction status updates; viewing is not account-gated. |
| Stale | Shows checked-on date, warning, and direct-confirmation advice where applicable. |
| Conflicting | Shows neutral status without choosing an unsupported winner. |
| Web provider failure | Local group remains usable and correctly labelled. |

## Desired and undesired behavior

| Desired | Undesired |
|---|---|
| Source/date/status are field-level. | One blanket “verified” badge. |
| Missing evidence is `Unknown`. | Inferred freshness or provenance. |
| Sensitive claims include caveats. | Allergy or safety guarantees. |
| Fresh session is local-only. | Automatically mixing web results. |
| Opted-in web results are source-grouped. | Visually indistinguishable providers. |
| Stale data remains visibly dated. | Hiding age behind vague “recent” text. |
| Conflicts are represented neutrally. | Silently selecting a preferred claim. |
| Corrections enter review. | Publishing user input immediately as fact. |
| Detail is available to guests. | Requiring an account to inspect provenance. |
| Dates are localized but machine-readable. | Ambiguous numeric-only dates. |

## Proposed data and API contracts

All interfaces are proposed and require reconciliation with inspected live contracts.

```text
TrustField<T> {
  value: T | null
  status: confirmed | reported | pending_review | stale | conflicting | unknown
  checkedOn: ISODate | null
  sources: [{ sourceId, displayName, sourceType, observedOn? }]
  caveatKey?: string
}

CorrectionSubmission {
  placeId, fieldKey, proposedValue, explanation?
  evidenceRefs?: UploadedEvidenceRef[]
  locale, consentVersion
}
```

- Proposed `GET /api/places/{id}/trust`.
- Proposed `POST /api/places/{id}/corrections` with idempotency protection.
- Source IDs must not expose private contact details, credentials, internal notes, or provider tokens.
- Freshness thresholds must be configured per field type and documented by content governance.
- Canonical shared URLs contain city, neighbourhood, query, category, filters, scope, locale only.

## Technical implementation plan

### Governance and data

1. Inspect existing schemas, providers, ingestion jobs, editorial workflows, and terms.
2. Inventory decision-relevant fields and identify lawful source, owner, and refresh policy for each.
3. Define evidence standards for each proposed status; remove statuses that cannot be governed.
4. Define field-specific stale thresholds and conflict-resolution procedures.
5. Map legacy nulls and untraceable values to `Unknown`, not `Confirmed`.
6. Establish correction triage, audit retention, abuse handling, and escalation.

### Frontend

7. Create reusable trust metadata and caveat components with localized plain language.
8. Show a compact summary on cards and complete field-level detail on place pages.
9. Keep source/status semantics available without relying on color or icons.
10. Group local and opted-in web results with accessible headings.
11. Render loading, unknown, stale, conflicting, error, and retry states.
12. Preserve discovery criteria and focus when opening/closing source details.
13. Use semantic dates with localized visible values and machine-readable ISO values.

### API, privacy, and security
14. Reconcile proposed contracts with current APIs and version migrations safely.
15. Validate status transitions server-side and authorize moderation separately from submissions.
16. Sanitize correction text and evidence; scan uploads and restrict file types/sizes.
17. Minimize correction personal data and state retention/deletion terms before submission.
18. Exclude correction evidence and moderation notes from public URLs and analytics.
19. Rate-limit submissions and use idempotency to avoid duplicates.

### Quality and rollout
20. Verify WCAG 2.2 AA and Dutch-English parity for statuses, caveats, dates, and errors.
21. Backfill only from authentic evidence; otherwise display `Unknown`.
22. Roll out by field group, audit sampled records, monitor unknown/stale/conflict rates, and provide rollback.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **BR-07-AC-01:** Every displayed decision-relevant field has visible source, checked-on date, and status, or explicit `Unknown`.
2. **BR-07-AC-02:** Status meanings are defined in plain Dutch and English and do not imply unsupported verification.
3. **BR-07-AC-03:** Sensitive claims show adjacent caveats and never guarantee allergy safety or other safety outcomes.
4. **BR-07-AC-04:** Fresh local-only sessions require explicit opt-in for web results, which are grouped by source.
5. **BR-07-AC-05:** Stale and conflicting records are visibly distinguished with dates and recovery guidance.
6. **BR-07-AC-06:** Trust metadata loading/error does not silently convert fields to confirmed.
7. **BR-07-AC-07:** Guests can inspect source details without account, map, or location permission.
8. **BR-07-AC-08:** Corrections are validated, privacy-disclosed, idempotent, and pending review rather than auto-published.
9. **BR-07-AC-09:** Shared URLs contain validated public criteria and no evidence, tokens, private preferences, or coordinates.
10. **BR-07-AC-10:** Back/cancel/retry retain unaffected discovery criteria and submitted-correction safety.
11. **BR-07-AC-11:** Trust information meets WCAG 2.2 AA and Dutch-English parity.
12. **BR-07-AC-12:** Freshness policy and status transitions are enforced per field and auditable internally.
