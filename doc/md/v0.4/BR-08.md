# BR-08 — Clear privacy information and controls

## Enhancement assessment

**Priority:** P0 — Release gate

**Criticality:** Critical

**Complexity:** Very high

**Estimated effort:** 15–25 person-days (provisional)

This estimate covers the full existing implementation specification, not only copy: just-in-time location explanation, web-processing disclosure, consent and denial states, privacy-safe URLs, retention/deletion controls, guest and account rights journeys, accessibility, localization, and acceptance testing. Risk is critical because unclear controls or state handling can expose private information or invalidate informed choice; complexity is very high because UI, storage, provider disclosure, authentication, and deletion behavior must agree end to end. P0 is a proposed release gate for affected v0.4 journeys, not a verified production emergency or user-approved deadline.

**Work packages**

- Privacy UX and policy-to-system mapping: **4–7 person-days**
- Location, web-scope, and storage-control implementation: **4–6 person-days**
- Guest/account rights and deletion-flow integration: **4–7 person-days**
- Privacy, accessibility, localization, and state QA: **3–5 person-days**

**Estimation basis and exclusions:** The range excludes global rollout or dataset acquisition, ongoing verification operations, and third-party or legal lead times. Existing core search, authentication, and data contracts are presumed reusable; if they are absent or materially incompatible, re-estimate. Person-days are combined design, engineering, and QA effort, not elapsed delivery time, and overlap across BRs must not be summed blindly. ## Objective

Give consumers timely, understandable control over location use, online-search scope, storage, retention, and deletion while preserving useful guest discovery when consent or permission is withheld.

## Scope

- Just-in-time geolocation explanation and explicit **Use my location** action.
- Manual neighbourhood alternative before and after permission denial.
- Explicit current Hague-only availability, fresh-session local-only search, and explicit **Include web results** opt-in.
- Source grouping and disclosure of relevant external processing before opt-in.
- Privacy center covering active controls, retention periods, deletion, and contact/escalation routes.
- Guest and account data-rights journeys.
- Privacy-safe canonical URLs and history.

## Non-goals

- Claiming consent or legal compliance without system and policy verification.
- Requesting precise location on page load.
- Requiring an account for browsing, manual neighbourhood search, or privacy information.
- Encoding location coordinates, tokens, or private preferences in shared URLs.
- Promising immediate deletion where a lawful, disclosed exception applies.
- Defining undisclosed analytics, advertising, or provider behavior.

## Privacy principles
1. Collect and transmit only data necessary for the selected action.
2. Explain processing before or at the moment of choice.
3. Keep browsing available when optional processing is refused.
4. Separate geolocation permission from map display and web-search scope.
5. Do not treat browser permission as broad consent for unrelated processing.
6. Make withdrawal as easy as activation.
7. State concrete retention periods and deletion exceptions only after system inspection and policy approval.
8. Avoid dark patterns, preselected optional controls, and bundled choices.

## Target screens

1. **Search form:** local-only default, unchecked **Include web results**, and scope explanation.
2. **Location chooser:** manual neighbourhood list and **Use my location** with just-in-time notice.
3. **Privacy center:** purposes, data categories, recipients, retention, controls, deletion, and contact route.
4. **Guest data controls:** clear local history/preferences and explain browser-held data.
5. **Account privacy:** export/deletion entry, identity confirmation, status, cancel where possible, and completion.
6. **Provider error/permission denial:** non-blocking recovery with manual/local alternatives.

## Interaction flows

### Flow 1 — Fresh local search
1. **Entry:** Guest or account consumer opens a fresh session.
2. **UI state:** Search scope is local-only; web-results opt-in is not preselected.
3. **Action:** Consumer searches.
4. **UI response:** Local results load without geolocation or web-provider transmission.
5. **Persisted state:** Public criteria enter canonical history; private preferences do not.
6. **Back/retry:** Criteria persist through history and local request retry.

### Flow 2 — Include web results
1. **Entry:** Consumer opens scope explanation.
2. **UI response:** Purpose, data sent, recipient categories/names where applicable, and policy link appear before activation.
3. **Action:** Consumer explicitly enables **Include web results**.
4. **Success:** Web request runs and results appear in a separate source group.
5. **Cancel/withdraw:** Disabling stops future web requests and removes that group without resetting local criteria.
6. **Error/retry:** Provider failure affects only web results; retry requires the scope to remain explicitly enabled.

### Flow 3 — Use my location
1. **Entry:** Consumer sees manual neighbourhood selection first.
2. **Action:** Consumer selects **Use my location** after reading the concise purpose.
3. **UI response:** Browser permission prompt occurs only now.
4. **Success:** Location resolves to editable discovery context; raw coordinates are not placed in URL.
5. **Denied/unavailable:** Message explains the outcome and focuses the manual alternative.
6. **Cancel/retry:** Existing criteria remain; Retry is consumer-initiated.

### Flow 4 — Clear guest data
1. **Entry:** Guest opens privacy controls.
2. **UI response:** Interface distinguishes browser-held data from server-held submissions, if any.
3. **Action:** Guest selects categories to clear and confirms.
4. **Success:** Selected local data is removed and confirmation names what was cleared.
5. **Cancel:** Nothing changes.
6. **Error:** Failure is explicit; UI does not claim deletion.

### Flow 5 — Account deletion request
1. **Entry:** Signed-in consumer opens account privacy.
2. **Action:** Consumer reviews effects, disclosed retention exceptions, and verifies identity proportionately.
3. **Submit:** Request receives a reference and status.
4. **Cancel:** If processing has not passed the documented cutoff, cancellation is confirmed.
5. **Complete:** Consumer receives confirmation describing deleted, anonymized, or lawfully retained categories.
6. **Error/retry:** No duplicate requests; context and status remain available.

### Flow 6 — Sign in from guest discovery
1. **Entry:** Guest browses, then chooses favourites, which are account-backed only.
2. **Action:** Consumer signs in after a clear value/privacy explanation.
3. **Success:** Public search context is preserved; optional private preferences sync only as disclosed.
4. **Cancel/failure:** Guest browsing and criteria remain intact.

## State requirements

| State | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Initial | Local-only, no geolocation request, optional controls unselected. |
| Loading | Name the active local/web/location/deletion operation; do not broaden processing. |
| Success | Confirm the selected action and scope without implying unrelated consent. |
| Empty | Explain no data/results without silently switching provider or scope. |
| Error | Identify affected processor/action, retain safe criteria, and provide Retry. |
| Permission denied | Preserve manual neighbourhood and list discovery. |
| Guest | Full browsing/searching/sharing plus relevant browser-data controls. |
| Account | Additional export/deletion/preferences controls with identity protection. |
| Withdrawal | Future optional processing stops; resulting UI state is clear. |
| Deletion pending | Reference, scope, timing, cancellation rule, and status are visible. |
| Deletion complete | Accurate category-level outcome; no false absolute claim. |

## Desired and undesired behavior

| Desired | Undesired |
|---|---|
| Fresh session starts local-only. | Preselected or silent online scope. |
| Web search follows explicit informed opt-in. | Sending query to a provider before choice. |
| Results are grouped by source. | Hiding external processing in mixed results. |
| Geolocation follows **Use my location**. | Permission request on page load or map open. |
| Manual neighbourhood always remains available. | Blocking discovery after denial. |
| Map loads only after **Show map**. | Treating map display as location consent. |
| Public URL has public criteria only. | Coordinates, private preferences, IDs, or tokens in URL. |
| Retention uses approved concrete periods. | Vague “as long as needed” copy without policy. |
| Deletion reports exceptions accurately. | Claiming deletion before completion. |
| Guest browsing survives sign-in cancel/failure. | Losing context or forcing registration. |

## Proposed data and API contracts

These contracts are proposed and must be reconciled with actual APIs, vendors, and policy.

```text
PrivacyNotice {
  version, locale, purposes[]
  dataCategories[], recipients[]
  retentionRules[], rights[], contactRoute
}

DeletionRequest {
  requestId, accountId, requestedAt
  categories[], status, cancellableUntil?
  completionSummary?
}
```

- Proposed `GET /api/privacy/notice?locale=en|nl`.
- Proposed `GET /api/account/privacy` for authenticated account controls.
- Proposed `POST /api/account/deletion-requests` with reauthentication and idempotency.
- Proposed `GET /api/account/deletion-requests/{id}` and `POST .../{id}/cancel`.
- Existing account/data-rights contracts take precedence until safely migrated.
- Provider calls must be audited so opt-in is enforced server-side as well as visually.
- Canonical URL permits city, neighbourhood, query, category, filters, scope, locale only after validation.

## Technical implementation plan

### Discovery and policy inventory
1. Inspect live client/server routes, storage, cookies, logs, analytics, map, geolocation, web providers, identity, backups, and support workflows.
2. Build an approved processing inventory: purpose, data, legal basis, recipient, location, retention, and deletion path.
3. Reconcile interface copy with actual provider terms and organizational policy.
4. Define concrete retention schedules and lawful exceptions; do not invent periods in UI.

### Frontend
5. Default fresh sessions to local-only and render optional online scope unselected.
6. Add concise scope disclosure plus accessible expanded details before opt-in.
7. Keep local results available when web scope is disabled or fails.
8. Add manual neighbourhood selection and gate browser permission behind **Use my location**.
9. Keep map independent and lazy-load it only after **Show map**.
10. Build privacy center, guest clear-data flow, and account deletion status screens.
11. Preserve public criteria across sign-in, cancel, errors, and browser history.
12. Implement initial, loading, success, empty, error, denied, pending, and completed states.

### Backend and integration
13. Enforce scope server-side; prevent provider requests without explicit request scope.
14. Avoid persisting raw coordinates unless a separately approved purpose requires it; never put them in shared URLs.
15. Validate/allowlist URL values and strip tokens, account IDs, and private preferences.
16. Implement deletion orchestration across primary stores, processors, logs, and backups according to approved policy.
17. Protect deletion with proportionate reauthentication, authorization, audit events, idempotency, and abuse controls.
18. Ensure analytics do not receive raw location, full sensitive queries, permission status, or deletion details unless approved and necessary.

### Accessibility, localization, rollout
19. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for notices, choices, dialogs, status, focus, contrast, reflow, and target size.
20. Release Dutch and English copy together with semantic parity and localized policy versions.
21. Test with provider/map calls monitored to prove no premature requests.
22. Roll out behind flags; verify data maps and deletion dry runs in non-production-safe conditions before exposure.
23. Monitor opt-in failures, permission errors, and deletion SLA against approved targets without profiling individuals.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **BR-08-AC-01:** Every fresh session starts local-only with web results unselected.
2. **BR-08-AC-02:** Web-provider processing occurs only after explicit informed opt-in and results are source-grouped.
3. **BR-08-AC-03:** Geolocation permission occurs only after **Use my location** and never from page/map load.
4. **BR-08-AC-04:** Manual neighbourhood and list discovery remain usable after permission denial or map/provider failure.
5. **BR-08-AC-05:** Privacy information identifies actual purposes, data, recipients, retention, controls, deletion, and contact route in both locales.
6. **BR-08-AC-06:** Withdrawal stops future optional processing without resetting unaffected search criteria.
7. **BR-08-AC-07:** Canonical URLs validate public criteria and exclude coordinates, private preferences, account IDs, and tokens.
8. **BR-08-AC-08:** Guest controls accurately clear browser-held data and never claim unperformed server deletion.
9. **BR-08-AC-09:** Account deletion is authorized, idempotent, status-visible, and reports lawful retention exceptions accurately.
10. **BR-08-AC-10:** Sign-in success, cancel, and failure preserve guest discovery context.
11. **BR-08-AC-11:** Privacy controls and states meet WCAG 2.2 AA and Dutch-English parity.
12. **BR-08-AC-12:** Network/storage inspection confirms no premature web-provider or geolocation processing.
