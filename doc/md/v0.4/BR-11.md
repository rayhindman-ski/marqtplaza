# BR-11 — Clear account and registration value

## Enhancement assessment

**Priority:** P2 — Normal

**Criticality:** Medium

**Complexity:** High

**Estimated effort:** 10–15 person-days (provisional)

This estimate covers the full existing implementation specification, not only copy: truthful account-value presentation, guest boundaries, contextual registration/sign-in, favourites, session expiry, safe return paths, privacy/retention/deletion explanation, responsive accessibility, localization, and acceptance testing. Risk is moderate because unclear value or broken detours undermine trust and task continuity; complexity is high because authentication lifecycle, persisted favourites, privacy states, and initiating context must remain synchronized.

**Work packages**

- Account-value and auth-journey UX design: **2–3 person-days**
- Registration, sign-in, session, and return-path implementation: **3–5 person-days**
- Favourites, account state, and privacy integration: **3–4 person-days**
- Accessibility, localization, and auth-state QA: **2–3 person-days**

**Estimation basis and exclusions:** The range excludes global rollout or dataset acquisition, ongoing verification operations, and third-party or legal lead times. Existing core search, authentication, and data contracts are presumed reusable; if they are absent or materially incompatible, re-estimate. Person-days are combined design, engineering, and QA effort, not elapsed delivery time, and overlap across BRs must not be summed blindly. [Assessment definitions and estimation basis](./requirements.md#assessment-definitions-and-estimation-basis)

## Status and interpretation

This document specifies proposed requirements; it does not describe implemented or verified behavior.
The live consumer application, identity provider, session model, account APIs, consent records, and deletion processes are unknown and must be inspected during implementation.
The workspace report viewer and weekend guide are not the live consumer application.
Proposed interfaces and delivery targets must be reconciled with actual contracts and policy before development.

## Exact business requirement

> MarqtPlaza must explain the consumer benefit of registration and having an account rather than rely on the visible “UserRole user” control to communicate account value.

## Objective

Explain, at the moment it is relevant, what a consumer gains from an account and what remains available without one.
Preserve complete guest browsing for search, neighbourhood browse, results, details, public actions, and sharing.
Restrict persisted favourites to authenticated accounts and communicate that boundary before authentication.
Return consumers to their initiating search/result context after sign-in, registration, cancellation, retry, or session recovery.
Make privacy, retention, and deletion consequences understandable in Dutch and English.

## Scope

- Navigation account entry, contextual favourite prompt, sign-in, registration, signed-in account summary, sign-out, and deletion entry point.
- Account benefit copy grounded in features that are actually implemented.
- Account-backed favourites and any other inspected, approved account feature.
- Guest/account state distinctions, session expiry, auth errors, and safe return paths.
- Privacy explanation for required and optional registration data.
- Mobile-first and accessible auth presentation.
- Dutch-English copy parity and Hague context retention.

## Non-goals

- Blocking guest access to discovery, list results, details, public outbound actions, or public share links.
- Advertising personalization, alerts, saved searches, or cross-device sync unless each capability exists and is verified during implementation.
- Implementing social identity providers not supported by the inspected identity stack.
- Storing guest favourites as if they were durable account records.
- Putting auth tokens, account identifiers, coordinates, or private preferences into shared URLs.
- Changing legal retention/deletion terms without policy approval.
- Treating the text “UserRole user” as adequate account value communication.

## Account value proposition

The minimum proposed account benefit is durable, account-backed favourites when that capability is implemented.
Benefit statements must name only inspected and working capabilities.
Suggested structure: **“Create an account to save favourites and access them when you sign in. You can continue browsing without an account.”**
If cross-device access is technically supported, it may be stated precisely; otherwise it must not be implied.
Privacy copy must distinguish data required to operate the account from optional preferences.
Deletion copy must state the actual deletion process, timing, exceptions, and contact route after policy and backend inspection.

## Target screens

1. **Guest navigation:** labeled **Sign in** or **Account** entry with brief benefit context, replacing ambiguous role text.
2. **Contextual favourite prompt:** benefit, account requirement, sign-in/register choices, and Continue browsing/Cancel.
3. **Sign-in:** identifier, credential method supported by the real identity stack, help, privacy link, submit, and registration path.
4. **Registration:** minimum required fields, purpose, validation, terms/privacy links, and clear submit.
5. **Authenticated account summary:** identity-safe greeting, favourites entry, privacy controls, sign-out, and deletion entry.
6. **Favourites list:** account-backed records, empty/loading/error states, and return to discovery.
7. **Session-expired state:** explanation, preserved public context, and reauthentication.
8. **Deletion flow:** consequences, confirmation, accessible error/success state, and policy-aligned outcome.

## Interaction flows

### Flow 1 — Guest explores account value

1. **Entry:** Guest opens the labeled account navigation item.
2. **UI response:** Product explains current verified account benefits and explicitly says browsing works without an account.
3. **Action:** Consumer chooses Sign in, Register, or Continue browsing.
4. **Continue/cancel:** Overlay/route closes and returns focus/context to its trigger.
5. **Back:** Browser Back returns to the exact preceding public route without losing criteria.
6. **Persisted state:** No account record or marketing consent is created merely by viewing the explanation.

### Flow 2 — Guest attempts to favourite

1. **Entry:** Guest activates **Save to favourites** on a result card/detail.
2. **UI response:** Prompt states that favourites are account-backed and explains the concrete benefit.
3. **Action:** Consumer selects Sign in or Register.
4. **Cancel:** Consumer returns to the same result; search criteria, scope, locale, and scroll context remain.
5. **Success:** After confirmed authentication, the initiating favourite request is safely completed or explicitly re-confirmed.
6. **Error:** Failure does not show a false saved state and offers Retry.
7. **Persisted state:** Favourite exists only when the authenticated account write succeeds.

### Flow 3 — Registration

1. **Entry:** Consumer chooses Register from an account-value or favourite prompt.
2. **Initial UI:** Only required fields for the inspected identity implementation are shown; optional fields are marked optional.
3. **Action:** Consumer submits.
4. **Validation:** Field-specific errors are associated with fields and summarized when helpful; entered non-secret values remain.
5. **Success:** Confirmation reflects the real verification/session process without claiming completion prematurely.
6. **Persisted state:** Required account data and consent evidence follow approved purpose/retention rules.
7. **Back/cancel:** Returns to initiating context without creating an account.
8. **Retry:** Network/server error is explicit and does not duplicate accounts.

### Flow 4 — Sign in and return

1. **Entry:** Consumer arrives from navigation, favourite, or session-expiry prompt.
2. **Action:** Consumer completes the identity method supported by the real stack.
3. **UI response:** Submission is disabled only while necessary; progress is announced.
4. **Success:** Server-confirmed session starts and safe return target restores city, neighbourhood, query, category, filters, scope, locale, and relevant result context.
5. **Failure:** Generic credential error avoids account enumeration and supports retry.
6. **Cancel:** Public guest route remains usable.
7. **Security:** Return destinations are internal/allowlisted and cannot create an open redirect.

### Flow 5 — Manage favourites

1. **Entry:** Signed-in consumer opens Favourites.
2. **Loading:** Existing content structure remains stable and status is announced.
3. **Success:** Account-backed items show current result data plus source/date/status/unknown handling required by BR-07.
4. **Action:** Consumer opens or removes a favourite.
5. **Remove response:** Confirmation/undo behavior follows inspected product policy; server success determines persisted state.
6. **Empty:** Explains how to browse and save without implying previously saved records.
7. **Error/retry:** Existing confirmed items are not silently discarded.

### Flow 6 — Session expiry

1. **Entry:** An authenticated write receives a confirmed expired/invalid session response.
2. **UI response:** Consumer is told that sign-in is required again; public browsing remains visible.
3. **Action:** Consumer reauthenticates or cancels.
4. **Success:** Safe context restores and pending destructive actions are not replayed automatically.
5. **Cancel:** Consumer stays in guest mode with public criteria preserved.
6. **Error:** Repeated failure does not trap focus or create a redirect loop.

### Flow 7 — Sign out and deletion

1. **Entry:** Signed-in consumer opens account controls.
2. **Sign out:** Confirmed session termination returns to a public route and retains only public search criteria.
3. **Delete action:** Consequences, actual retention exceptions, and authentication requirements are explained.
4. **Confirm:** Deletion request uses the real policy/API and reports processing status accurately.
5. **Cancel:** Account and context remain unchanged.
6. **Error/retry:** No success claim appears until the backend confirms the defined state.
7. **Afterward:** Tokens/private preferences are removed from client state according to inspected policy.

## Desired and undesired behavior

| Area | Desired behavior | Undesired behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Guest access | Full public discovery remains available | Registration wall before search/results |
| Value | Concrete, implemented benefit | Vague role label or unbuilt feature promises |
| Favourite | Account-backed and server-confirmed | Fake guest persistence or optimistic false success |
| Context | Search/result restored after auth/cancel | Return to generic homepage with criteria lost |
| Identity | Labeled Sign in/Account control | “UserRole user” as consumer-facing explanation |
| Data | Minimum necessary fields and clear purposes | Unexplained optional data collection |
| Privacy | Actual retention/deletion route stated | Unsupported instant-deletion promise |
| Errors | Retryable, enumeration-safe, non-destructive | Reveal account existence or erase inputs |
| URL | Public criteria only | Tokens, account IDs, coordinates, preferences |
| Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA target and managed focus | inaccessible modal, timeout, or errors |
| Localization | Complete Dutch-English auth copy | Mixed-language identity or legal controls |
| Session | Public browsing survives expiry | Redirect loop or blocked discovery |

## Required state behavior

### Initial guest

- Use a clear **Sign in** or **Account** label, not an unexplained role value.
- State concrete benefits and that browsing can continue without registration.
- Do not initialize location/map to communicate account value.

### Initial account

- Display only the minimum identity needed in shared/public contexts.
- Provide favourites, privacy, sign-out, and deletion routes only when actually available.
- Never expose private attributes in page metadata or shared URL.

### Loading

- Announce authentication, registration, favourite, and deletion progress.
- Avoid duplicate submissions while preserving navigation/cancel where safe.
- Never represent an optimistic favourite or deletion as final before confirmation.

### Success

- Restore safe originating context.
- Confirm state change with non-color-only text.
- Keep canonical URL limited to validated city, neighbourhood, query, category, filters, scope, and locale.

### Empty

- Favourites empty state explains how to discover and save.
- It does not imply records were lost or fabricate examples as saved data.

### Error

- Distinguish validation, credentials, session, network, favourite-write, and deletion-request errors.
- Preserve non-secret input and public context.
- Avoid account enumeration and raw backend messages.

### Permission denied

- Browser location/map permission has no effect on registration, sign-in, favourites access, or public discovery.
- If storage/cookie restrictions prevent auth, explain the limitation and recovery without coercion.

### Guest/account transitions

- Sign-in/register cancel returns to guest browsing.
- Successful auth restores context through validated server/client state, not sensitive URL parameters.
- Sign-out removes private client state while retaining public criteria only.

## Technical implementation plan

### Discovery

1. Inspect identity provider, session cookies/tokens, registration fields, verification, favourites storage, consent records, deletion process, and auth routes.
2. Confirm which account benefits currently work before approving any copy.
3. Threat-model return URLs, CSRF, XSS, session fixation, enumeration, and favourite authorization.

### Frontend

4. Replace ambiguous consumer-facing role text with a labeled account state control while preserving required administrative semantics outside consumer UI.
5. Build reusable account-value content for navigation and contextual favourite prompts.
6. Implement accessible form labels, autocomplete attributes, validation associations, error summary, progress status, and focus restoration.
7. Store pending public context separately from secrets and validate it before navigation.
8. Render favourite state from confirmed account data; reconcile optimistic UI on failure if optimism is retained.
9. Keep guest search/list/detail/share routes independent of auth.
10. Ensure mobile dialogs/sheets reflow, permit zoom, and avoid virtual-keyboard obstruction.

### Proposed contracts

11. Reconcile all current identity/favourite contracts before adopting these proposed interfaces.
12. Proposed account summary: `accountId` retained server-side, display-safe profile fields, capability flags, and locale.
13. Proposed favourite record: stable result identifier, created timestamp, and account association; source facts remain resolved through discovery data.
14. Proposed favourite operations: authenticated list/create/delete with idempotency and authorization checks; exact paths depend on live stack.
15. Proposed safe return state: signed/validated internal route plus public criteria, never credentials, tokens, coordinates, or private preferences.
16. Proposed errors use stable codes suitable for localized client messages and never expose raw provider details.

### Integration and privacy

17. Use secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookies where compatible with inspected architecture; do not move secrets into URLs.
18. Apply CSRF protection and server-side ownership checks to favourite and account changes.
19. Minimize registration fields and separate required service processing from optional consent.
20. Reconcile privacy notice, retention, export/access, sign-out, and deletion with BR-08 and actual operations.
21. Log only approved security events; redact credentials, tokens, queries combined with identity, and location data.
22. Make deletion status truthful: requested, processing, completed, or failed according to the real backend.

### Accessibility and localization

23. Target WCAG 2.2 AA for account entry, dialogs, forms, timeouts, errors, focus, contrast, reflow, and status messages.
24. Do not impose inaccessible cognitive tests; ensure recovery methods are understandable.
25. Add Dutch and English strings together, including validation, privacy, session, favourite, and deletion states.
26. Preserve locale across auth while using canonical internal Hague identifiers and locale-appropriate place naming.

### Rollout

27. Gate rewritten prompts/forms if feature flags exist, without changing authorization boundaries.
28. Test with guest, new, returning, expired-session, deletion-pending, and restricted-storage states.
29. Measure privacy-approved aggregate prompt continuation/cancel, auth failure, context restoration, and favourite-write failure; set thresholds during implementation.
30. Provide rollback that preserves account data and avoids orphaned favourites.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **BR-11-AC-01:** Guest consumers can search, browse neighbourhoods/lists, view details, use public actions, and share without registering.
2. **BR-11-AC-02:** Every registration prompt states at least one verified account benefit and explicitly offers continued guest browsing.
3. **BR-11-AC-03:** Consumer navigation uses an understandable account label/state and does not rely on “UserRole user” to convey value.
4. **BR-11-AC-04:** Favourites persist only as account-backed, server-confirmed records and never appear successfully saved after a failed write.
5. **BR-11-AC-05:** Sign-in, registration, cancellation, retry, and session expiry preserve the originating public criteria and result context.
6. **BR-11-AC-06:** Safe return handling rejects external/invalid destinations and never places tokens, account IDs, coordinates, or private preferences in URLs.
7. **BR-11-AC-07:** Registration requests only justified fields, identifies optional fields, and links to accurate privacy/retention/deletion information.
8. **BR-11-AC-08:** Authentication errors are localized, actionable, and do not reveal whether an account exists.
9. **BR-11-AC-09:** Sign-out clears private client state while preserving only public search criteria.
10. **BR-11-AC-10:** Deletion UI reports actual requested/processing/completed/failed state and never claims completion prematurely.
11. **BR-11-AC-11:** Account, auth, favourite, expiry, and deletion journeys meet the WCAG 2.2 AA target.
12. **BR-11-AC-12:** All account-related copy and states have complete Dutch-English parity.
13. **BR-11-AC-13:** Location/map denial does not block account access, favourites, or guest discovery.
14. **BR-11-AC-14:** Current Hague-only availability and search scope survive account round trips without unsupported coverage claims.
