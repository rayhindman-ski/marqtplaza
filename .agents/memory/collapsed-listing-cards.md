---
name: Collapsed listing cards
description: Defines the information priority for collapsed discovery cards.
---

Collapsed listing cards must show a meaningful description summary alongside the primary timing and price facts. Secondary utilities such as calendar export, evidence, and routing belong in the expanded state.

**Why:** Hiding context while showing large utility controls forced users to open each card just to understand what the listing was about.

**How to apply:** Keep a concise two-line summary visible by default, remove its clamp when expanded, and defer secondary actions until expansion.
