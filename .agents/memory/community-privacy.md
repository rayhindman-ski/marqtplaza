---
name: Community contribution privacy
description: Privacy boundaries for the neighbourhood community layer.
---

Community contributions are public as local, moderated messages—not as public user profiles. Do not expose author identities, direct messages, follower graphs, or open comment threads in the first social layer.

**Why:** The product is intended to feel like a safe local village square, where residents can help and inform one another without being pushed into a generic social-network identity model.

**How to apply:** Keep resident-facing participation aggregate and privacy-safe. Extend moderation and content quality before considering identity-led or one-to-one social features.
